Robert M. Zinke Energy Management Program

The first and largest energy management program in the nation, founded in 1958 at OU's Price College of Business. It draws from Business, Geosciences, Engineering, and Law — and is one of the few undergraduate programs accredited by the American Association of Petroleum Landmen (AAPL), with a direct focus on land, mineral acquisition, and energy finance. Nearly all EMSA members graduate with full-time positions already in hand.
