Common questions

Before you call

How much does a coated floor cost?

Every floor is priced after we see it. The honest answer is that the condition of your concrete drives the final number more than anything else. Grinding, crack and spall repair, moisture mitigation and stripping an old coating can move an estimate a long way in either direction, and two identical 2-car garages can quote very differently. Send us your project details above and we'll confirm a real number with a free on-site measure and inspection.

How long does installation take?

Most residential floors are ground, repaired and coated in a single visit. The floor is ready to be used about 24 hours after the service is completed.

Can you coat floors in the winter?

In the winter, coatings have to be done indoors. Garages and basements are no problem year-round, since they can be enclosed and heated during application. Outdoor surfaces like pool decks and patios are scheduled for the warmer months.

Do I need to prep the concrete myself?

No. We diamond-grind every slab to a clean mechanical profile and fill cracks or pitting before coating. No acid wash, no shortcuts, no peeling later.
